Master's Thesis: Passive Investing & Active Alpha

Examines whether the growth of passive investing (from ~6% to 51% of U.S. mutual fund AUM, 1996–2025) is associated with changes in aggregate active manager alpha. Uses ~29,800 active and ~3,700 passive funds from Morningstar Direct with CAPM, Fama–French 3- and 5-factor models. Documents a specification pitfall in fee-spread controls, finds no robust linear association between passive share and alpha under multifactor models, and provides exploratory evidence of stress-conditional effects on alpha during periods of high passive concentration.

Empirical Finance Grossman–Stiglitz Fama–French Python Econometrics Morningstar Direct

Methodology

A Python-based composite rating model with Bayesian updating from actual race results. The 2026 season introduces major regulation changes—a new 50/50 ICE/electric power unit and new aero—so pre-season priors carry significant uncertainty that gets resolved as races are completed.

Pre-season prior (5 factors):

  • 2025 WDC points (25%) — Final championship standings, normalized. Source: formula1.com
  • Testing pace (20%) — Fastest lap per driver, Bahrain Test 2. Source: the-race.com
  • Team reliability (10%) — Total pre-season km completed (proxy for car maturity). Source: the-race.com
  • Reg-change adaptability (20%) — Historical constructor performance in the first year of major rule changes (2009, 2014, 2017, 2022)
  • Team trajectory (25%) — Blend of 2025 constructor strength (40%) and testing pace (60%)

Bayesian update from race results:

  • Race evidence — Points scored, average finishing position, and reliability (DNF rate) across completed 2026 races
  • Dynamic weighting — Race data weight scales with √(races/24). After 1 race: ~15%. After 12: ~52%. After 24: ~73%

Prior scores use softmax (temp=0.15) with a 35% regulation uncertainty discount compressing toward uniform. Race evidence is blended via weighted average. After Round 1, Russell's Australia win pushes him from 3rd to 1st. As the season progresses, the prior fades and results dominate.

Personal analytical project. Source code and data in the repository. Re-run python f1_model/predict_2026.py after each race to update.
