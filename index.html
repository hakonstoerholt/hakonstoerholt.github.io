About BI Energy and Commodities

BI Energy and Commodities is a student society at BI Norwegian Business School dedicated to exploring the energy sector, sustainability, and future business opportunities within these fields. We organize events, workshops, and company visits to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry practice.

Our mission is to create awareness about energy-related challenges and innovations while providing our members with valuable connections to industry professionals and potential employers.

Our Vision

To become the leading student organization for energy and sustainability in Norwegian business education, preparing the next generation of business leaders for the green transition.

Our Values

  • • Sustainability and environmental responsibility
  • • Innovation and forward-thinking
  • • Collaboration across disciplines
  • • Practical, industry-relevant learning
